Bumper Ball Privacy Policy

Operated by MiaM, contact: miam.akpinar[at]gmail[dot]com.

What we collect

  • The name you type in. It is shown to other players in your room and on the hall of fame.
  • A random player ID created on your device so your wins are counted to you. It is not linked to your phone, email or any account.
  • Game results: wins, goals and rounds played per room code, who scored on whom (for the nemesis and revenge features), your XP and level, and the pattern, hat and trail you chose.

What we don't collect

No email address, no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising IDs and no tracking. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only to run the game and show the hall of fame. It is stored on the game server and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Email miam.akpinar[at]gmail[dot]com with your player name and room code and we will remove your results. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes your player ID from your device.

Children

Bumper Ball is meant for workplace use and is not directed at children under 13.
